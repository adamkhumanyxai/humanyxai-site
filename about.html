About HumanyxAI

Twenty years of enterprise delivery, working for you directly.

HumanyxAI is one operator, not an agency. Behind it is a twenty-year career deploying complex systems inside some of Australia's largest organisations — thousands of projects, the full weight of enterprise governance, stakeholder politics and all.

That experience is now pointed at a specific, narrow problem: making AI actually useful in the two places it keeps getting oversold — how a business runs internally, and how it talks to its customers.

The independence is deliberate. No account managers, no resold platforms, no bench to keep busy. You brief the person who does the work, and the person who does the work answers for it.

Human by design.
