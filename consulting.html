What the audit covers

Five things, done properly.

01

Current-state assessment

The tools you already pay for, the workflows they touch, and where the gaps actually are — not where the brochure says they are.

02

Stakeholder interviews

The people who'll live with the result, asked before anything is decided. Not just the person signing.

03

Tool fit analysis

Vendor-agnostic, always. We don't resell platforms, so the recommendation has no commission hiding in it.

Written recommendation

A plain-English position you can act on with us, with another vendor, or on your own. The truth even when the truth is "you don't need us."

05

Roadmap

Sequenced by impact and effort, so the first week of work pays for the second.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Are you going to sell us your own tools at the end?+
We build voice agents, yes — and the audit is still vendor-agnostic. If the right answer is an off-the-shelf tool that isn't ours, that's what the recommendation says. "You don't need us" is a finding we're happy to charge for.
How long does an audit take?+
Days to weeks, depending on how many stakeholders and systems are in scope. The timeline is agreed up front, and you get the written position either way.
What do we get at the end?+
The written recommendation and the roadmap. Yours to keep, act on, or ignore — it doesn't expire if you walk away.
