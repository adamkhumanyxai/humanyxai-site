FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do we even need AI? (Be honest.)+
Maybe not. That's what the audit is for — and "you don't need us" is a finding we're happy to charge for, because it saves you far more than it costs.
Will callers know they're talking to AI?+
Yes — our agents disclose themselves. Deceiving callers is both an ethical line and a commercial mistake: trust collapses the moment someone feels tricked.
What happens when the agent can't answer?+
It says so, takes a detailed message, and flags a human callback. Graceful hand-off is a designed behaviour, not a failure mode.
Where is our data stored?+
In Australia, on Australian infrastructure. Full stop.
How long does a deployment take?+
An audit is days to weeks. A voice agent is typically live in two to four weeks, depending on integrations. A forward-deployed engagement is scoped per site.
What does it cost?+
The audit is a fixed, published price. Voice agents have a transparent entry tier. Forward-deployed engagements start at $30,000 AUD — and come with ROI reporting that shows whether it paid for itself.
